## Observations on Overfitting

Increasing the maximum depth allows a Decision Tree to learn more detailed patterns in the training data. However, a very deep tree may memorize training examples instead of learning general patterns.

If training accuracy increases while testing accuracy stays the same or decreases, it may indicate overfitting. A smaller maximum depth can help the model generalize better.

The best depth should be selected using reliable validation results rather than training accuracy alone.
