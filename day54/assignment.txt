## Part 5: Theory

**Bagging:** Random Forest builds multiple decision trees independently and combines their predictions.

**Boosting:** Gradient Boosting and XGBoost build trees sequentially. Each new tree attempts to correct errors made by previous trees.

**GridSearchCV:** Tests combinations of hyperparameters using cross-validation and identifies the best combination according to the selected scoring metric.
