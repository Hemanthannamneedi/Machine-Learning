## Concepts Learned

**Hidden State:** The internal memory of an RNN that carries information from previous time steps to the next time step.

**Vanishing Gradient:** During backpropagation through long sequences, gradients can become extremely small, making it difficult for the network to learn long-term dependencies.

**Exploding Gradient:** Gradients can become excessively large during training, causing unstable weight updates.

**LSTM:** Long Short-Term Memory is an advanced RNN architecture designed to handle long-term dependencies more effectively.

## Conclusion
Three RNN architectures with 8, 16, and 32 hidden units were trained and compared. Their performance was evaluated using MSE, MAE, and RMSE. The experiment demonstrates how RNNs process sequential information and use hidden states to predict future values.
